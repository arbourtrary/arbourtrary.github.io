<h2>Company Anagrams</h2>
<p><b>Inspiration</b>: My brain is a jumble of parenting, tech news and word games. One day in my boredom I thought about the company name Vercel. It seemed odd, and I wondered where that name came from. Then realized <b>Vercel</b> is an anagram of <b>clever</b>. I can't verify if that was intentionally done by their hired branding agency? But it made me wonder about other companies.</p>

<p><b>Description</b>: To figure out if other companies were anagrams like this, I pulled together some data sources and filtered for (ideally / mostly) companies people actually recognize. The list is biased towards tech: the 1,000 largest US-listed companies by market cap from the SEC, companies with articles on 25 or more Wikipedias from Wikidata, Y Combinator's top companies, and a handful of other sources.</p>

<p><b>Method</b>: English words come from <a href="http://wordlist.aspell.net" target="_blank">SCOWL</a>'s standard-size dictionary, filtered out if they're not in the 70,000 most common words in Peter Norvig's counts from the Google Web Trillion Word Corpus.</p>
